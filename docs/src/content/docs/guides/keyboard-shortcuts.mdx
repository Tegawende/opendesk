Almost everything in opendesk works without a mouse. Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in the app, outside a text box, to see every shortcut. The sheet lists the shortcuts of the page you are on first, and you can search it.

<Screenshot src="/guides/shortcuts/sheet" alt="The Keyboard shortcuts sheet with a search box, the shortcuts of the page you are on first, then every other group" />

Menus and tooltips show the keys of their action too, and the command menu (<kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>k</kbd>) can run any shortcut by name: type what you want to do.

<Callout type="note" title="Reading the keys">

<kbd>mod</kbd> is <kbd>⌘</kbd> on macOS and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> elsewhere. Two keys joined by "then" are pressed one after the other: <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>p</kbd> means press <kbd>g</kbd>, let go, then press <kbd>p</kbd>. Single-key shortcuts are paused while you type, so <kbd>r</kbd> in a message is just a letter.

</Callout>

## Change your shortcuts

Open **Settings › Shortcuts**, or press <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>?</kbd>.

1. Find the shortcut: search by its name, its keys or where it works.
2. Click its keys and press the new ones. A key then another, like <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>o</kbd>, works too.
3. If another shortcut already uses those keys where both work, opendesk says which one and keeps the old keys.

Each shortcut has **Reset**, to go back to its default keys, and **Turn off**. **Reset all** puts every shortcut back. Your keys are yours: they follow you to every workspace and every device you sign in on, and nobody else's change.

The keys of the editor and the canvas, like <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>b</kbd> for bold, are listed for reference and can't be changed.

## In the reply box

- Type <kbd>/</kbd> to insert a [saved reply](/guides/saved-replies-and-views/), and <kbd>@</kbd> in a note to mention a teammate.
- <kbd>enter</kbd> picks the highlighted item in those lists, <kbd>esc</kbd> closes them.
- A click in a link places the cursor to edit it; <kbd>mod</kbd> click opens it in a new tab, to check where it goes before sending.

## In the command menu, menus and pickers

Use the arrow keys to move and <kbd>enter</kbd> to choose, or <kbd>mod</kbd> <kbd>enter</kbd> to open a result in a new tab with the command menu left open. <kbd>esc</kbd> closes without changing anything. Results are links, so a right click, a <kbd>mod</kbd> click or a middle click opens them in a new tab too.

In the wiki's own page list, the arrow keys move between pages, <kbd>→</kbd> and <kbd>←</kbd> open and close a page's subpages, and <kbd>enter</kbd> opens one. <kbd>alt</kbd> with an arrow key moves the focused page. In the graph, the arrow keys move it, <kbd>+</kbd> and <kbd>-</kbd> zoom and <kbd>0</kbd> fits it.

</Fold>
